# Support Resources

Thank you for using `anef-tracker`! Here is how to get help:

- **Documentation**: Check out our [Technical Documentation](docs/architecture.md), [Configuration Manual](docs/configuration.md), and [TUI Guide](docs/tui-guide.md).
- **Troubleshooting**: Review [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md) or run `anef doctor`.
- **GitHub Discussions**: Ask questions or share workflows in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/anef-tracker/anef-tracker/discussions).
- **Bug Reports & Feature Requests**: Open an issue using our [Issue Templates](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/).
